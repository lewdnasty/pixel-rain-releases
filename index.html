PIXEL RAIN

v0.48.0-beta.5 · a cascading rainbow of pixels for your browser, phone and desktop

Looks & color

  • 16 one-click looks, from synthwave to snowfall
  • Save your own looks, share them by link, or start with one
  • 9 palettes, or colours from your own image
  • Grayscale, b&w, random hue and rainbow drift
  • Plaid: fixed, still or woven into the rain
  • Trippy warped backgrounds and rainbow wings
  • Adjustable bloom and day tint
  • Text rain in any font, Zalgo optional

Physics & elements

  • Sand piles, water pools, fire burns
  • Paint walls and spectrum-splitting prisms
  • Hold to attract, release to fling
  • Paddle mode: every catch splits in two
  • 48 tuning sliders, plus shuffle

Desktop

  • Rain lands on windows and icons (Wallpaper Engine)
  • Dragged windows carry or shove the rain
  • Monitor seams act as walls
  • Separate screens, each with its own settings, or synced
  • Screen weather per monitor
  • Helper reads rectangles only, nothing leaves your PC

Music

  • Reacts to system audio, mic or audio files
  • Pulse, generation, intensity and speed
  • Automatic in Wallpaper Engine

Phone & web

  • Tap to burst, drag to part the rain
  • Tilt to tip, shake to splash
  • Installs as an offline app
  • Respects reduced motion
  • BIG BANG landing splash, title fit, big or huge
  • Splash taglines, or write your own
  • Tutorial for every control, then random combos

Power & sharing

  • Battery saver and frame cap
  • Idle mode shows the time
  • Stamps: your words, the clock, a picture or a shuffled folder
  • One link shares your exact setup
  • Live stats and a 10 s benchmark
